Responsible gambling

GoalPre is for information and entertainment only. 18+.

GoalPre is not a bookmaker and does not accept bets. Our predictions are statistical estimates, not advice to gamble and not a guarantee of any result. If you choose to gamble, do so only with a licensed operator, only if it is legal where you live, and only if you are 18 or older.

Gamble only for fun, never for income

Betting should be entertainment, not a way to make money or solve financial problems. The maths is always in the operator’s favour over time. Treat any stake as the cost of entertainment that you are fully prepared to lose.

Simple rules that keep it safe

  • Set a budget before you start, and never exceed it.
  • Only stake what you can afford to lose — never money meant for essentials.
  • Never chase losses. Trying to win back a loss is the most common path to a bigger one.
  • Set time limits and take regular breaks.
  • Don’t gamble when upset, bored, stressed or under the influence of alcohol.
  • Keep it balanced — don’t let it crowd out family, work or other interests.

Warning signs

Consider taking a break and seeking support if you:

  • bet more than you planned, or more than you can afford;
  • chase losses or borrow money to gamble;
  • hide your gambling, or feel guilty or anxious about it;
  • find it hard to stop even when you want to.

Where to get help

Free, confidential support is available 24/7. You are not alone, and reaching out early makes a real difference.

  • BeGambleAware — free advice and the National Gambling Helpline (UK): 0808 8020 133.
  • GamCare — support, information and counselling.
  • Gamblers Anonymous — peer-support meetings worldwide.
  • GAMSTOP — free self-exclusion from UK-licensed gambling sites.
  • National Council on Problem Gambling (US) — call or text 1-800-522-4700.

If you are outside these regions, search for the licensed problem-gambling helpline in your own country.

Tools offered by licensed operators

Reputable, licensed bookmakers offer deposit limits, loss limits, time reminders, cooling-off periods and self-exclusion. Use them — they exist to keep gambling under control.
