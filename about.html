About GoalPre

An independent statistical football-prediction project.

GoalPre is an independent project that turns publicly available football match data into clear, probability-based predictions. We are football and data enthusiasts, not a bookmaker. Our goal is simple: to show the maths behind a match in a way that is honest, transparent and easy to read, so that supporters can understand why one result is more likely than another instead of just being handed a “tip”.

What makes us different. Every number on this site is produced by a model and explained in plain English. Nothing is hand-picked, nothing is hidden, and we never claim certainty — football is unpredictable, and our figures are probabilities, not promises.

What we do

For every upcoming fixture in the leagues we cover, GoalPre publishes:

  • 1X2 probabilities — the chance of a home win, draw or away win.
  • Most likely correct score — the single scoreline the model rates highest.
  • Over/Under 2.5 goals — the chance of a high- or low-scoring game, with expected total goals.
  • Value and confidence signals — where the model disagrees with the market, and how strongly our models agree with each other.

Our three prediction engines

We deliberately show more than one model, because no single method is perfect. You can switch between them on the predictions page:

  1. Poisson model — a Dixon-Coles-corrected goal model that converts each team’s attack and defence strength into a full score grid.
  2. KPI framework — a stats-weighted confidence index built from win rate, venue form, recent form, head-to-head record and average goals.
  3. AI consensus — an ensemble that blends the Poisson model, the KPI model and the de-margined market odds into one probability, then writes a short plain-English insight for the match.

Our full method is documented on the predictions guide and how-it-works pages.

Where our data comes from

Fixtures, results, team data and bookmaker odds are sourced from professional sports-data providers (via the API-Football service). We take reasonable care to present this data accurately, but we do not control the source and cannot guarantee it is error-free. Team names, league names and logos remain the property of their respective owners and are used for identification only.

How often the site updates

GoalPre refreshes automatically several times a day. Each update pulls the latest fixtures and results, recalculates every prediction, records a snapshot of each tip so we can measure accuracy honestly, and republishes the site. The “Results & Accuracy” page shows how previous predictions actually performed — we never edit history after the fact.

Who the site is for

GoalPre is for football fans who enjoy the analytical side of the game: following form, comparing models and understanding probability. The content is intended for information, study and entertainment. It is not betting advice, and it is intended for adults aged 18 or over. If you choose to gamble, please read our responsible gambling page first.

Editorial standards

  • We publish the method behind every prediction.
  • We report accuracy from saved, time-stamped snapshots — not after-the-fact edits.
  • We clearly label estimates as estimates and never guarantee a result.
  • We separate our own model output from third-party data and odds.
