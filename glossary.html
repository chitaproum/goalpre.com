Football betting & stats glossary

Plain-English definitions of the terms used across GoalPre.

New to prediction markets or statistical models? This glossary explains the terms you will see on GoalPre and across football analytics in general. For a step-by-step walkthrough, read the predictions guide.

Match-result terms

1X2
The match-result market with three outcomes: 1 home win, X draw, 2 away win. The three probabilities add up to 100%.
Double chance
A single bet covering two of the three 1X2 outcomes (e.g. “home win or draw”). Safer, but at shorter odds.
Draw no bet
A bet on home or away where your stake is returned if the match is drawn.
Correct score
A prediction of the exact final scoreline. GoalPre shows the single most likely score from the model’s grid.

Goals markets

Over/Under 2.5 goals
Will the two teams combine for three or more goals (Over) or two or fewer (Under)? The “.5” avoids a tie — there is no “exactly 2.5 goals”.
Both teams to score (BTTS)
A market on whether both sides find the net at least once.
Expected goals (xG)
An estimate of how many goals a team “should” score given the quality of its chances. On GoalPre, “expected goals” refers to the model’s own λ (lambda) output, not shot-based match xG.
Expected total goals
The sum of both teams’ expected goals — a quick read on whether a game projects high- or low-scoring.

Odds & probability

Decimal odds (“Coef”)
The European odds format. Your total return per unit staked, including the stake. Odds of 2.50 return 2.50 for every 1 staked.
Implied probability
The chance suggested by the odds: 1 ÷ decimal odds. Odds of 2.00 imply 50%.
Margin (overround / vig)
The bookmaker’s built-in edge. The implied probabilities of all outcomes add up to more than 100%; the surplus is the margin.
De-margined odds
Odds with the margin stripped out, so the probabilities sum to exactly 100% — useful for comparing a market fairly with a model.
Value
When your estimated probability is higher than the odds imply. A positive edge over the long run — never a guarantee on one match.

Models & method

Poisson distribution
A probability model for counting rare, independent events — ideal for goals. It turns an expected-goals figure into the chance of 0, 1, 2… goals.
Dixon-Coles correction
A well-known adjustment to the basic Poisson model that corrects the probabilities of low scorelines (0–0, 1–0, 0–1, 1–1), which plain Poisson slightly misprices.
Attack / defence rating
How many goals a team scores or concedes relative to the league average. Above 1.0 attack = scores more than average.
Home advantage
The small, consistent boost home teams enjoy, applied to the home side’s expected goals.
KPI confidence index
GoalPre’s stats-weighted score built from win rate, venue form, recent form, head-to-head and average goals.
Consensus / ensemble
Combining several models into one blended probability, which is often more stable than any single model alone.

Responsible-gambling terms

Stake
The amount of money risked on a bet. Only ever stake what you can afford to lose.
Chasing losses
Betting more to try to win back losses — a classic warning sign of problem gambling. Don’t.
Deposit / time limits
Tools offered by licensed operators to cap how much you can deposit or how long you play. Use them.
