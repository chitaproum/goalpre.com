Frequently asked questions

The questions we are asked most often about GoalPre.

Are GoalPre predictions free?

Yes. Every prediction, every league and every tool on the site is free to use. The site is supported by advertising and optional sponsored offers.

How are the predictions made?

They are generated automatically by statistical models — a Poisson goal model, a KPI confidence framework and an AI-consensus blend. Nothing is hand-picked. The full method is explained on the predictions guide.

How accurate are the predictions?

We publish our real track record on the Results & Accuracy page, calculated from saved, time-stamped snapshots of each tip. No model is perfect: a 60% pick is still expected to lose about four times in ten. Judge any model over the long run, not on a single match.

Do the predictions guarantee a win?

No. Football is unpredictable and our figures are probabilities, not promises. GoalPre is for information and entertainment, not a guarantee of any result.

What is the difference between the Poisson, KPI and AI engines?

The Poisson engine models goals directly; the KPI engine weights form, venue and head-to-head; the AI engine blends both with the market odds and writes a short summary. You can switch between them with the “Data” toggle on the predictions page.

What does “Coef” mean?

“Coef” is the decimal (European) odds for the displayed tip. You can switch the display to fractional, American or implied-probability format in Settings. See the glossary for more.

Where does your data come from?

Fixtures, results and odds come from professional sports-data providers (via API-Football). We present it as accurately as we can but do not control the source. See About for details.

How often is the site updated?

Several times a day. Each run refreshes fixtures and results and recalculates every prediction.

Which leagues do you cover?

Dozens of competitions worldwide, from major European leagues to many domestic leagues and cups. Use the league filter on the predictions page to browse them.

Can I use GoalPre to bet?

GoalPre does not accept bets and is not a bookmaker. If you choose to gamble elsewhere, that is entirely your decision and risk. Please read our responsible gambling page first and only stake what you can afford to lose. You must be 18 or older.

Do you store my personal data?

The site is a static website and does not require an account. Please see our Privacy & Cookie Policy for how advertising and analytics may use cookies.

How can I contact you?

See the contact page or email contact@goalpre.com.
